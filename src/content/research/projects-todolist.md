<p>TodoApp is a user-friendly and feature-rich task management application that helps users stay organized, increase productivity, and effortlessly manage their tasks and to-do lists.</p>
<h2 id="todoapp">TodoApp</h2>
<p>TodoApp is a powerful and intuitive task management application designed to help you stay organized and productive. With its simple yet effective features, you can easily create, manage, and track your to-do lists and tasks.</p>
<h3 id="features">Features</h3>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>Create and Manage Lists</strong>: TodoApp allows you to create multiple lists, enabling you to organize your tasks based on different projects, categories, or priorities. Each list can have its own set of tasks, providing a structured approach to task management.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Add and Edit Tasks</strong>: Adding tasks to your lists is a breeze. Simply enter the task title, description, and due date to get started. You can also edit and update task details as needed, ensuring that your task list remains up to date.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Set Due Dates and Reminders</strong>: Stay on top of your deadlines by setting due dates for your tasks. TodoApp provides the option to set reminders, ensuring that you never miss an important deadline again. The reminders help you stay focused and complete your tasks in a timely manner.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Mark Tasks as Done</strong>: Once you complete a task, you can mark it as done. This feature allows you to track your progress and visually see the tasks you have accomplished. It provides a sense of achievement and motivates you to continue working on your tasks.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Organize Tasks with Tags</strong>: TodoApp allows you to add tags to your tasks, making it easy to categorize and filter them based on different criteria. By using tags, you can create custom labels for your tasks and quickly find specific tasks when needed.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Move Tasks Between Lists</strong>: If your tasks need to be reorganized or belong to a different list, TodoApp lets you move tasks seamlessly between lists without losing any data. This flexibility ensures that you can adapt your task organization based on changing priorities or project requirements.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>User-Friendly Interface</strong>: The application provides a clean and intuitive interface that makes task management a hassle-free experience. The menu-based system allows for easy navigation and quick access to all the essential features, making it simple for users of all skill levels to use and navigate the app effectively.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Data Persistence</strong>: TodoApp ensures that your task data is securely stored and saved, so you can pick up where you left off even after closing the application. Your tasks and lists remain intact, allowing you to resume your work without any data loss or interruption.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="how-to-use">How to Use</h3>
<p>Using TodoApp is simple and straightforward. Just follow these steps:</p>
<ol>
<li>Create a new list for your tasks or choose an existing list to add tasks.</li>
<li>Add tasks to the selected list by providing the necessary details such as title, description, and due date.</li>
<li>Organize your tasks within the list by rearranging them as needed.</li>
<li>Mark tasks as done when you complete them to keep track of your progress.</li>
<li>Edit or update task details whenever required.</li>
<li>Move tasks between lists if they need to be reclassified.</li>
<li>Utilize tags to categorize and filter your tasks based on different criteria.</li>
<li>Set reminders for important tasks to receive timely notifications.</li>
<li>Enjoy a clutter-free and organized task management experience!</li>
</ol>
<p>TodoApp provides a comprehensive solution for individuals and teams who want to stay organized and increase their productivity. Whether you’re a student managing assignments, a professional handling multiple projects, or a busy individual juggling personal tasks, TodoApp simplifies task management and helps you stay on top of your responsibilities.</p>
